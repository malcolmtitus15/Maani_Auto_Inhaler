Safety checks

Safeguards from the “Safety and Reliability” slide. Change from the deck: the daily cap is now a warning, not a block, so a severe attack is never refused.

A sharp blow then a brief gasp. Should be rejected.

Blowing into the mouthpiece. Wrong direction, should be ignored.

A second dose within 5 s is blocked. This is only minimum spacing: it never limits total puffs.

Six inhales just past the lockout (about 40 s). Every dose still fires; after the 4th, a heavy-use warning and an alert to the doctor and caregiver appear.

Illustrative simulation built from the BreathSync deck. The 0.20 kPa threshold, 120 ms validation window, 5 s lockout, 4-dose usual daily maximum (a warning only, never a block) and 20-dose low warning are demo values, not tested specifications. Real values are for the technical team and a clinician to confirm. The simulator assumes a dose released by hand is not counted, because the deck does not describe a way to detect it.
